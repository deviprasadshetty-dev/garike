About the story

When life tries to bury her, Gauri grows again.

Malenadu's mist, coastal hardship, betrayal, social judgement, and motherhood shape the emotional journey of Garike. At the center is Gauri, an ordinary woman forced into an extraordinary fight for her child and her own place in the world.

The novel follows her from humiliation to strength, asking how a woman called unwanted can one day become the person others turn toward for dignity, food, and courage.
